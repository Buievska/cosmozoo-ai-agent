# CosmoZoo Instagram Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines the Instagram content strategy for CosmoZoo Production.

The AI should use this strategy when creating:

* Content calendars
* Reels ideas
* Stories
* Carousels
* Captions
* Hooks
* Campaign concepts
* Content series
* Engagement ideas
* Lead-generation content
* Instagram growth recommendations

The strategy should evolve based on real performance data, audience behavior, current projects, and business priorities.

---

# 1. Main Instagram Goals

CosmoZoo's Instagram should support the following goals:

### Brand Awareness

Make more relevant people aware of CosmoZoo and what the company creates.

### Portfolio

Show the quality, creativity, and variety of CosmoZoo's work.

### Trust

Demonstrate professionalism through real projects, behind-the-scenes content, process, expertise, and consistency.

### Lead Generation

Turn Instagram attention into:

* DMs
* Project inquiries
* Calls
* Collaborations
* Website visits
* Client relationships

### Brand Positioning

Position CosmoZoo as a multidisciplinary creative production partner rather than only a photography or video provider.

---

# 2. Target Audience

Primary audiences include:

* Brands
* Small and medium-sized businesses
* Startups
* Fashion brands
* Beauty brands
* Lifestyle brands
* Restaurants
* Hospitality businesses
* Artists
* Musicians
* Creators
* Influencers
* Entrepreneurs
* Agencies
* Companies building or redesigning their digital presence

Geographic focus:

* Los Angeles
* California
* United States
* International clients when relevant

The exact target audience may change depending on the current business strategy.

---

# 3. Content Pillars

Content should be distributed across several pillars.

## Pillar 1 — Portfolio & Finished Work

Show the final result.

Examples:

* Photography projects
* Commercial videos
* Brand campaigns
* Reels
* Website projects
* Web design
* Sound production
* Creative campaigns
* Social media content

Purpose:

* Demonstrate quality
* Build credibility
* Give potential clients a visual reference
* Show the range of services

---

## Pillar 2 — Behind the Scenes

Show how the work is created.

Examples:

* Photoshoots
* Video production
* Camera setups
* Lighting
* Studio moments
* Sound recording
* Editing
* Web development
* Creative direction
* Location scouting
* Team collaboration
* Before/after transformations

Purpose:

* Humanize the brand
* Build trust
* Demonstrate expertise
* Make the production process interesting

---

## Pillar 3 — Creative Concepts

Show ideas before or beyond the final result.

Examples:

* Moodboards
* Visual references
* Creative concepts
* Storyboards
* Shot ideas
* Color directions
* Campaign concepts
* Website concepts
* Branding ideas

Purpose:

* Demonstrate creative thinking
* Differentiate CosmoZoo from simple production services
* Attract clients who value creative direction

---

## Pillar 4 — Education & Expertise

Teach useful things related to CosmoZoo's expertise.

Examples:

* Photography tips
* Video tips
* Reels advice
* Lighting explanations
* Social media content advice
* Branding tips
* Website UX/UI advice
* Web design mistakes
* Sound and audio tips
* Content strategy
* Production advice

Educational content should be practical and easy to understand.

Avoid generic advice that could come from any marketing account.

---

## Pillar 5 — People & Personality

Show the people behind CosmoZoo.

Examples:

* Team introductions
* Day in the life
* Production days
* Personal creative opinions
* Studio moments
* Funny or unexpected moments
* Team collaboration
* Creative process
* Personal stories connected to the work

Purpose:

* Make the company more human
* Build connection
* Increase trust
* Make the brand memorable

---

## Pillar 6 — Services

Explain what CosmoZoo can actually do.

Possible topics:

* Photography
* Video production
* Social media content
* Social media management
* Creative direction
* Web design
* Web development
* Audio and sound production
* Full creative production

Service content should focus on client problems and outcomes rather than simply listing services.

---

## Pillar 7 — Social Proof

Use real evidence whenever available.

Examples:

* Client projects
* Testimonials
* Reviews
* Published work
* Collaborations
* Case studies
* Client feedback
* Project results

Never create fictional testimonials, results, statistics, or client claims.

---

## Pillar 8 — Culture & Inspiration

Show the creative world around CosmoZoo.

Examples:

* Visual references
* Cinematic inspiration
* Photography references
* Interesting locations
* Creative trends
* Film references
* Design inspiration
* Music and sound inspiration
* Visual experiments

This content should reinforce the creative identity of the brand.

---

# 4. Content Mix

The Instagram feed should not consist entirely of sales posts.

A healthy content mix should combine:

* Portfolio
* Behind the scenes
* Creative concepts
* Education
* Personality
* Services
* Social proof
* Inspiration

The exact percentage should be adjusted based on performance.

The AI should avoid repeating the same content type too frequently.

---

# 5. Reels Strategy

Reels should be one of the main discovery formats.

Potential Reel categories:

### Project Reels

Show finished work.

### BTS Reels

Show the production process.

### Educational Reels

Share one useful idea or tip.

### Transformation Reels

Show:

* Before → after
* Raw → final
* Concept → result
* Setup → finished shot
* Design → website
* Raw audio → final audio

### Creative Reels

Use cinematic or visually interesting concepts.

### Personality Reels

Show the people and culture behind CosmoZoo.

### Opinion Reels

Share a creative perspective or professional opinion.

### Trend Adaptations

Use relevant trends only when they fit the CosmoZoo brand.

Never use a trend simply because it is popular.

---

# 6. Stories Strategy

Stories should maintain regular contact with the audience.

Possible Story sequence:

1. Hook
2. Context
3. Behind the scenes / value
4. Result
5. Interaction
6. CTA when relevant

Useful interactive formats:

* Polls
* Questions
* Sliders
* Quizzes
* This or That
* Reactions
* DM prompts

Stories can also be used to:

* Tease upcoming projects
* Show work in progress
* Announce launches
* Share availability
* Share new work
* Drive traffic to the website
* Start conversations with potential clients

---

# 7. Content for Lead Generation

Lead-generation content should answer questions potential clients may have.

Examples:

* What does working with CosmoZoo look like?
* What happens during a production?
* How does a brand photoshoot work?
* What does a website project include?
* How can professional content improve a brand's presence?
* What is the difference between a simple shoot and a full creative production?
* What can CosmoZoo handle from concept to final delivery?

Lead-generation content should educate first and sell second.

---

# 8. Content Series

The AI should develop recurring content series when useful.

Possible examples:

### Behind the Frame

Explain how a particular visual was created.

### From Concept to Final

Show the development of a project.

### CosmoZoo BTS

Behind-the-scenes production moments.

### Creative Breakdown

Break down a visual, campaign, website, or production decision.

### One-Minute Creative Tip

Short practical advice.

### Before / After

Show transformations.

### Meet the Team

Introduce people behind the work.

### Project Spotlight

Highlight one completed project.
